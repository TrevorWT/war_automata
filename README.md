# War Automata

War Automata is a planned medieval-themed Rust simulation in which two teams battle on a grid. Teams take turns, and their units follow simple movement and combat rules. Possible unit types include infantry, ranged units, commanders, and priests who can convert enemy units.

The project explores how unit rules, team composition, and starting positions produce outcomes such as formations, clustered fights, and stalemates. The rules are still being designed; see the [project proposal](docs/proposal.md) for the current plan.
